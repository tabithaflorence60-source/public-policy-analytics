## Standard for future videos

Each video should answer one clear question and follow a consistent structure:

1. policy problem
2. concept or theory
3. real sustainability/energy example
4. analytical or R demonstration where useful
5. interpretation for policymakers
6. one takeaway

This makes the YouTube channel, GitHub repository, dissertation work, and future teaching portfolio reinforce the same expertise.
