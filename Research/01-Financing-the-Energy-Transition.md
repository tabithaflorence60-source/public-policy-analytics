# Financing the Energy Transition

## Public Policy, Private Capital and Institutional Risk

### Research question

How do public policy, institutional quality, and financial structures influence the mobilization of capital for renewable-energy transitions?

### Analytical framework

1. **Capital need** — investment requirements across technologies and infrastructure.
2. **Sources of finance** — public finance, private capital, development finance institutions, blended finance, concessional finance, and domestic financial markets.
3. **Risk allocation** — policy, regulatory, currency, off-taker, construction, technology, and political risk.
4. **Institutional conditions** — regulatory credibility, utility performance, procurement, contract enforcement, tariff design, and intergovernmental coordination.
5. **Distributional outcomes** — affordability, energy access, public value, and transition costs.

### Research questions

- Which policy risks deter renewable-energy investment?
- When does public finance crowd in private capital?
- How do utility reform and tariff design affect investment readiness?
- How is risk allocated between governments, investors, utilities, and consumers?
- Which financing structures support investment and equitable access?
