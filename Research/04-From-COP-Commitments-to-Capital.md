# From COP Commitments to Capital

## How Global Climate Policy Shapes Energy Investment

### Research question

How do international climate commitments translate into national policy, financial flows, and implemented energy-transition projects?

### Core idea

Global climate agreements matter only if they alter incentives, institutions, and capital allocation. The pathway from a COP decision to a financed energy project passes through multiple stages: international commitments, national targets, domestic regulation, project pipelines, risk allocation, financing structures, procurement, and implementation.

### Analytical chain

**COP / Paris Agreement → NDCs and national targets → policy instruments → financial architecture → project pipeline → capital deployment → implementation outcomes**

### Research themes

- Paris Agreement implementation
- NDC ambition and credibility
- climate-finance commitments
- development-bank and multilateral finance
- national investment plans
- carbon markets and other policy instruments
- policy consistency across ministries and regulators
- private-sector response to public commitments
- accountability between international pledges and domestic implementation

### Emerging-market focus

Developing economies face a distinctive challenge: they are expected to expand energy access and infrastructure while also shifting toward lower-carbon systems. That makes the quality of international support, domestic institutions, and investment frameworks central to the effectiveness of global climate policy.

### Applied questions

- Which international commitments materially affect investment decisions?
- How do NDCs become investable project pipelines?
- What causes a gap between climate pledges and capital deployment?
- How do multilateral institutions reduce investment barriers?
- How should climate finance balance mitigation, adaptation, access, and development needs?

### Methods

The project can combine:

- COP and UNFCCC document analysis
- NDC comparison
- climate-finance tracking
- national policy and investment-plan analysis
- institutional mapping
- comparative case studies
- R-based analysis of commitments, flows, and outcomes

### Potential outputs

- a COP-to-capital policy map
- a comparative NDC-investment database
- a climate-finance flow visualization
- short policy briefs after major COP meetings
- a teaching module on climate diplomacy and sustainable investment
