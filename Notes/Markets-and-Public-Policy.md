# Markets and Public Policy

This topic examines how incentives, prices, scarcity, and market structure shape public-policy problems and government responses.

## Concepts
- scarcity and opportunity cost
- incentives
- supply and demand
- efficiency and public value
- regulation and public/private roles

## Application
Use energy, transportation, housing, infrastructure, or environmental services to examine when market outcomes align with public goals and when policy intervention may be justified.
