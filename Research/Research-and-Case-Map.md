# Research and Case Map

This repository uses applied research cases to deepen expertise in energy and sustainability policy without narrowing the course headings themselves.

## Core research thread

**Energy transitions as governance problems**

Key questions:
- How do institutions shape renewable-energy outcomes?
- How do policy design, infrastructure, finance, and public participation interact?
- How can quantitative evidence and R strengthen comparative policy analysis?
- How should resilience, equity, and justice be evaluated alongside efficiency?

## Core cases

### Kenya
- geothermal development and Olkaria
- grid reliability and renewable integration
- energy access and affordability
- institutional coordination among regulators, utilities, ministries, and communities
- public-private investment and infrastructure

### Portugal / European Union
- renewable integration and grid resilience
- multilevel governance across EU, national, and regional institutions
- sustainability policy implementation
- circular economy and local implementation
- comparative lessons for Kenya

| Course topic | Applied research use |
