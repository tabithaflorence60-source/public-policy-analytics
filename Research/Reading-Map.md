# Reading Map

This page maps research literature to the course topics. The purpose is not to reproduce course readings, but to use scholarship to strengthen original notes, cases, R exercises, and teaching videos.

## Public Goods and Externalities

**Useful literature and concepts**
- welfare economics and market failure
- climate change as a collective-action problem
- environmental externalities and pollution control
- common-pool resources

**Research application**
- emissions and transportation externalities
- climate mitigation
- electricity reliability and shared infrastructure
- multi-scale collective action
